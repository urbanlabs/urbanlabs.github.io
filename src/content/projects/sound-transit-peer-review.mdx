UrbanLabs contributed to Sound Transit's Innovation and Technology Program Strategic Plan, helping shape a practical approach to data, partnerships, and technology investments tied to the agency's broader mobility mission.

The draft strategic plan emphasized customer focus, stronger use of data and research, partnerships, open data, and smart technology that could improve rider information and operational performance. That framing aligns closely with the UrbanLabs belief that public-sector technology work should stay grounded in actual rider needs and agency realities.

This project reflects an important part of the UrbanLabs mission: helping public agencies learn, prioritize, and innovate in ways that make systems more usable for the people who rely on them.
