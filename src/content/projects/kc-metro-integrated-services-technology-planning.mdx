UrbanLabs supported King County Metro as a subject-matter expert while the agency explored how to evolve siloed technologies into a more cohesive system for non-fixed-route services.

The scope included advising on objectives, reviewing interview questions and draft deliverables, discussing key findings, and providing ad hoc support for planning technology that could support Metro's integrated services vision.

The project is notable because it focused on the connective tissue between systems: how booking, scheduling, dispatch, data integration, and customer experience should fit together in a way that supports a more consistent plan-book-pay experience.
