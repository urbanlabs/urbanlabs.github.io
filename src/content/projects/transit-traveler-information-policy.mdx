UrbanLabs evaluated current policy and developed approaches to transit traveler information for Cal-ITP, connecting technical realities with questions of implementation, governance, and rider experience.

This kind of work requires more than technical knowledge alone. It involves understanding where policy, standards, operational constraints, and human expectations meet, then helping organizations make choices that improve the public experience rather than adding complexity around it.

It is a strong example of the UrbanLabs approach: use technology and analysis seriously, but always in service of people trying to move through real places.
