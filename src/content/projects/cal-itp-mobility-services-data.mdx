UrbanLabs has led work on the Mobility Services Data Team for the California Integrated Travel Project, helping shape how transit data is produced, governed, and used in practice.

The work connects technical standards to public outcomes: clearer expectations for data quality, better traveler information, and stronger interoperability across systems that riders depend on every day.

This project is important because it treats technical infrastructure as public infrastructure. Data quality is not only a backend concern; it affects whether people can trust the information they receive and whether agencies can deliver a better transit experience at scale.
