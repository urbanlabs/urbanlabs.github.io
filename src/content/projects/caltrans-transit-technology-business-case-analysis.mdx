UrbanLabs contributed to transit technology business-case analysis work for Caltrans, with project materials organized around transit technology priorities such as APC, CAD/AVL, customer messaging systems, and transit signal priority.

Although the folder materials available here are partial, they clearly show UrbanLabs working within a broader effort to assess and articulate the case for transit technology investments across functional areas that influence operations, information quality, and service performance.

This kind of project matters because agencies need more than vendor promises or industry momentum. They need a way to think through priorities, benefits, tradeoffs, and implementation realities in a structured way.
