UrbanLabs supported Metro Transit on a coordinated software procurement effort centered on the agency's transit information mission: helping riders access timely, accurate, customer-friendly information so they can ride with confidence and ease.

The project materials describe a broad ecosystem of interdependent systems, including prediction engines, alerts management, real-time data concentrators, trip planning, schedule data editing, and detour data. UrbanLabs contributed to scope and requirements work across these connected tools.

This project is important because it treats procurement as part of product and systems strategy. The choices agencies make about interoperability, flexibility, and user experience in procurement shape what riders and staff can do for years afterward.
