Elizabeth is a former SFCTA executive and longtime advisor to agencies, research programs and open-source efforts. She does her best work across boundaries: between technical and executive teams, between policy goals and delivery realities, and between institutions that need to trust each other.

After years in Washington DC, Austin, San Francisco and Seattle, she now lives in Leavenworth, a walkable small town in the Cascade foothills, served by Amtrak and Link Transit and close to the mountains and rivers. Big-city systems and small-town life both shape how UrbanLabs thinks about what makes a place work.
